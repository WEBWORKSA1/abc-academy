Questions about the plan

Is the plan really free?

Yes. The plan uses ABC.Academy's free tools and printables. Tutors and programs are optional and you'll always see their prices before committing to anything.

How are tutors and programs chosen?

We look for qualified early-literacy and early-math educators and programs with clear pricing and good family feedback. You decide whether to contact any of them.

Will my information be sold?

No. We only share your request with partners if you tick the optional sharing box, and only to answer your request.

My child is struggling. Is this a diagnosis?

No. The plan is an educational starting point. If you're worried about speech, hearing, vision or development, talk to your pediatrician or your child's school.
