How to enter

  1. Create

    Your child makes a drawing, story, craft or short video on this month's theme. Grown-ups may write down a story the child tells.

  2. Upload

    Save a photo or video to Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or iCloud and copy a share link.

  3. Submit

    Fill in the form with the link. Use your child's first name or initials only.

  4. Judging

    Entries are judged in three age groups: 2–4, 5–6 and 7–8.

  5. Winners

    Winners are contacted by email within 14 days of the close and listed here by first name and age with permission.

    Official rules

    1. No purchase necessary. Entry is free. A purchase or donation does not improve the chance of winning.
    2. Eligibility. Open to children ages 2–8. Entries must be submitted by a parent, legal guardian or teacher aged 18 or older. Void where prohibited by law. Employees of ABC.Academy and their immediate families are not eligible.
    3. Entry period. Each contest runs from the first day to the last day of the calendar month, ending 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time. One entry per child per month.
    4. Judging. Winners are chosen by ABC.Academy judges using the published criteria. This is a contest of skill, not a random drawing. Judges' decisions are final.
    5. Prizes. One winner per age group unless stated otherwise. Prizes are not transferable and have no cash alternative except where required by law. ABC.Academy may substitute a prize of equal or greater value. Winners are responsible for any taxes.
    6. Notification. Winners are emailed within 14 days of the close and must reply within 14 days or an alternate may be chosen.
    7. Rights. Entrants keep ownership of their work. By entering you grant ABC.Academy a free, non-exclusive license to display winning entries with the child's first name and age on this website and our social channels.
    8. Privacy. We never publish a child's last name, school, location or face without separate written permission. See the privacy policy.
    9. Conduct. Entries must be original and family-friendly. We may disqualify entries that copy others' work, include personal information or break these rules.
