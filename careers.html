How we work

  • Kids first. Every lesson, story and game is judged by one question: does it help a child learn?
  • Small, focused projects. Most work is short, clearly scoped and paid per project.
  • Credit where it's due. Contributors are credited on the pages they help create.
  • Remote and flexible. Work from anywhere, on your own schedule.

What happens after you apply

  1. We review every application within 10 business days.
  2. Shortlisted applicants get a short, paid sample task.
  3. Successful contributors join our project roster.
