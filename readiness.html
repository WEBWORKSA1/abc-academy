Reading & school readiness check

Twelve quick questions, about five minutes. Answer based on what your child does most days. You'll get a snapshot of four skill areas and what to practice next.

Answers stay in your browser. Nothing is sent unless you choose to request a plan afterwards.

What this check looks at

The questions cover four areas that kindergarten and first-grade teachers commonly look for: knowing letters and their sounds, early reading skills like rhyming and retelling, number sense, and the fine-motor control and focus that writing needs.

Every child develops at their own pace, and a "not yet" today is normal — especially for younger children. Use the results to decide what to practice, not to label your child. Our kindergarten readiness checklist explains each skill in more depth.
