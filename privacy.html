Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

ABC.Academy ("we", "us") provides free early-learning tools for children ages 2–8, designed to be used with and by parents, guardians and teachers. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

Children's privacy

Children can use every tool, game, story and printable without an account, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Our forms are for adults (18+) only and say so. Contest entries are submitted by a parent, guardian or teacher and ask only for a child's first name or initials and age group. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Progress features (stars, mastered sight words, saved form drafts) are stored only in your browser's local storage on your device. We cannot see them, and you can clear them anytime by clearing your browser's site data.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (learning plan, contest entry, donation pledge, job application, partnership inquiry, newsletter or contact form), we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email, phone (optional), country and your message. We use this only to respond to your request, send what you asked for (for example your learning plan or newsletter), run contests and improve the site.

Form messages are delivered to us by email through a form-processing service (FormSubmit). That service processes the submission to deliver it and does not use it for its own marketing.

Tutor and program matching. If you request tutoring or program options and tick the optional sharing box, we may share your request (your first name, contact details, child's age range, focus areas, budget and timing) with up to three vetted learning partners so they can send you options. If you don't tick the box, we don't share it.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message, and ads may be non-personalized where consent is not given. Choosing "Necessary only" in our cookie notice requests non-personalized ads.

Embedded YouTube videos load only when you press play and use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com). Once a video plays, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

Fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver the font files.

How long we keep information

We keep form submissions for as long as needed to respond and for up to 24 months for records, job applications for up to 12 months, and newsletter subscriptions until you unsubscribe.

Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe from any email using the link in it.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete information you've sent, using the contact form (topic: Privacy request).
  • Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA, CCPA/CPRA or COPPA), you may have additional rights. We honor applicable requests.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we limit what we collect and share.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Significant changes will be highlighted on the home page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Use our contact form.
