About ABC.Academy

Every child deserves a strong start in reading, writing and math — whether or not their family can pay for an app subscription.

Why we built this

Most early-learning platforms put their best tools behind a monthly fee. Free alternatives often mean a pile of static PDFs. ABC.Academy sits in between: interactive, talking tools that feel like an app, printables you can make in seconds, and plain-language guides for the grown-ups — all free, with no account.

What we believe

  • Sounds before symbols. Children who can hear and play with sounds learn to read more easily, so our tools talk out loud.
  • Short and often. Ten focused minutes a day beats an hour once a week.
  • Grown-ups matter most. Our tools are designed to be used together, and our guides help parents and teachers know what to do next.
  • Privacy by default. Children never create accounts or enter personal information. Progress is saved only on your device.

How ABC.Academy is funded

The site is supported by advertising (Google AdSense), sponsorships, optional tutoring and program referrals for families who ask for them, and donations. These keep every tool free. Sponsored content is always labeled, and partners never get access to children's information.

Who builds it

ABC.Academy is an independent website run by a small team with help from freelance educators, writers, illustrators and developers. Want to contribute? See careers. Want to support the mission? See ways to give.
