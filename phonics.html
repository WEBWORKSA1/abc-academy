A simple order for teaching phonics

Phonics works best in a steady sequence where each step builds on the one before:

  1. Letter sounds. Children need quick, confident sounds for most single letters first.
  2. Blending CVC words. Consonant–vowel–consonant words like cat, pig and sun. Say each sound, then push them together.
  3. Word families. Change only the first sound (cat, hat, mat) so the rest feels familiar.
  4. Digraphs and blends. Letter teams like sh and ch, and blends like st and fr.
  5. Magic e and vowel teams. Long vowel patterns such as cake, kite and rain.

Ten minutes a day is plenty. Finish with a short read so new skills turn into real reading — try our decodable stories. For more detail, read how to teach blending step by step.
