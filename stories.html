How to read these stories together

  1. Listen first. Press Read to me once so your child hears a fluent reading.
  2. Read together. Read it again side by side, pointing to each word.
  3. Your child reads. Let them try alone. If they get stuck, prompt "What sound does it start with?" before you tell them the word.
  4. Talk about it. Answer the three questions, then ask your own: "What would you do?"

Reading the same story on three different days builds fluency faster than three new stories. More ideas in our read-aloud routines guide.
