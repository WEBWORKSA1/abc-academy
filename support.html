Donation questions

Is my donation tax-deductible?

ABC.Academy is not a registered charity, so donations are not tax-deductible. Your receipt will show the amount and purpose.

Can I cancel a monthly gift?

Yes, anytime. Reply to any receipt or use the contact page and we'll stop future payments.

Do donors get ad-free access?

Everything stays free for everyone. Supporters at any level receive a quarterly update on what their gifts built.
