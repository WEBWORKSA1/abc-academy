Getting the most from tracing sheets

  • Start at the top line. Most letters begin at the top. Building that habit early prevents backwards letters later.
  • Say it while tracing. "M says mmm" while writing M links the shape to the sound.
  • Short and often. One row done well beats a whole page done in a hurry.
  • Finish with a free row. The last row has no gray guide so your child can try alone.
