How we write our guides

Our guides are written by the ABC.Academy editorial team for parents and teachers of children ages 2–8. We draw on widely accepted early-literacy and early-math practice, keep advice practical and avoid jargon. Guides are reviewed regularly and updated when practice or evidence changes.

Guides are general education, not medical or psychological advice. Spot an error or want a topic covered? Tell us.
