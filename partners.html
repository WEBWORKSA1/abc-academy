Reach the families and teachers of young learners

ABC.Academy is where parents and teachers of children ages 2–8 come to teach reading, writing and math. Partner with us through sponsorships, contest prizes, featured placements, tutoring referrals and school programs.

Ways to partner

Every package is reviewed for fit with our audience. We only feature brands that are appropriate for families with young children.

Who visits ABC.Academy

  • Parents of children ages 2–8 looking for alphabet, phonics, sight word and math practice
  • Pre-K to Grade 2 teachers printing worksheets and using tools on classroom screens
  • Homeschooling families planning their week
  • Families seeking extra help through our learning-plan and tutor-match requests

Our standards

We don't accept advertising for products that are unsafe or unsuitable for families with young children, and we never sell children's personal information. Sponsored placements are always labeled. Display advertising on the site is served by Google AdSense under Google's policies.
