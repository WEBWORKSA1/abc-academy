Questions parents ask

Is ABC.Academy really free?

Yes. Every tool, game, story and printable is free with no account. The site is supported by advertising, sponsors and donations.

What ages is it for?

Children roughly 2 to 8 years old — toddlers learning letter names through second graders building reading fluency and math facts.

Do children need an account?

No. Children never sign up or enter personal information. Stars and progress are saved only in your browser on this device.

Can teachers use it in class?

Yes. The tools work on interactive whiteboards, and printables are free for classroom use. See our terms for details.

How do I get help for a struggling reader?

Start with the readiness check, then request a free learning plan. If you want, we'll also match you with tutors or programs that fit your budget.
